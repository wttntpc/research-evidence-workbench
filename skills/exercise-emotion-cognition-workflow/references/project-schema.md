# Project schema

Use this reference when preparing searches, literature matrices, or extraction forms for the exercise–emotion–cognition project.

## Two linked research tracks

1. **Current study**：college students／young adults; physical activity, fitness, sedentary behavior, or body composition; emotion／mood; cognition／executive function; initially cross-sectional.
2. **Long-term program**：acute and chronic exercise prescription; emotion–cognition interaction; HRV／EEG／ERP mechanisms; causal experimental and intervention designs.

Do not mix the tracks into one undifferentiated search or infer causal mechanisms from the cross-sectional track.

## Topic groups

| Code | Group |
|---|---|
| A | 急性運動 × 情緒／心情 |
| B | 急性運動 × 情緒—認知交互作用 |
| C | 身體活動／體適能 × 情緒／認知 |
| D | 身體組成／靜態行為 × 情緒／認知 |
| E | HRV／EEG／ERP 機制 |
| F | 測量效度、任務信度與方法品質 |

The groups are defaults, not permanent taxonomy. Merge or split them only after the researcher confirms.

## Search concept blocks

- Exercise/body：exercise, physical activity, physical fitness, sedentary behavior, body composition, body fat, muscle mass, aerobic exercise, resistance exercise.
- Emotion：emotion, mood, affect, valence, arousal, emotion regulation, anxiety, stress.
- Cognition：cognition, executive function, inhibitory control, working memory, cognitive flexibility, attention, planning, Go／No-Go, Stroop, Flanker.
- Population：college students, university students, undergraduate students, young adults.
- Mechanism：HRV, heart rate variability, EEG, ERP, event-related potential, autonomic regulation, N2, P3.

## Full-text extraction order

Use consistent structured text in the matrix:

- Design/sample：design｜total n｜group n｜age｜sex／gender｜health/training status｜eligibility｜comparator.
- Instruments：activity/fitness｜body composition｜emotion instrument｜HRV equipment/metrics｜EEG equipment/components.
- Task/intervention：exercise type｜intensity｜duration｜frequency/weeks｜measurement timing｜cognitive task｜primary outcome.
- Findings：effect estimate＋95% CI／effect size｜interaction/mediation｜null findings｜page/table.
- Relevance：reusable design｜limitation to avoid｜variable to add｜specific use for the current study.

## Minimum evidence gate

A candidate may support proposal prose only after it has a stable identifier, full-text design and outcome details, an effect estimate or clearly stated result, a page／table locator, limitations, and an explicit verification state.
